The Etymology of Utility

Conceptualized for the Philippine climate, Kallos Gorgon draws its foundation from classical Greek etymology: Kallos (κάλλος), denoting visual harmony and refined proportion, and Gorgon (Γοργών), symbolizing resilience and bold physical presence[cite: 10].

Powered by Quadros Technology™, our garments utilize a precise cotton-polyester matrix to optimize thermoregulation, maximize airflow, and maintain structural integrity against tropical moisture, eliminating the compromise between aesthetic sophistication and functional performance[cite: 10, 11].
